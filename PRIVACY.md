# Privacy Policy for Zest

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

Zest ("the Application") is developed with privacy as a foundational principle. We believe that your clipboard contents are sensitive, personal, and belong exclusively to you.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
- Zest **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal data, usage metrics, telemetry, or analytics.
- Zest contains **zero tracking SDKs, zero advertising frameworks, and zero analytics libraries**.

## 2. 100% Local On-Device Execution
- The Application operates **completely offline**. It does not establish network connections or send data to external servers.
- All clipboard history, pinned clips, and user preferences are stored **strictly on your local device** in macOS secure application directories (`~/Library/Application Support/Zest` or the macOS App Sandbox container).

## 3. Privacy & Security Guard
- Zest actively inspects system pasteboard metadata to protect your privacy:
  - **Password Managers & Ephemeral Clips:** Clips flagged with `org.nspasteboard.TransientType`, `org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType`, or `org.nspasteboard.AutoGeneratedType` are automatically ignored and never saved to history.
  - **Application Blacklist:** Users can designate specific applications (e.g., password managers, terminal sessions) whose clipboard contents will be permanently ignored.
  - **RAM-Only Ephemeral Mode:** Users can toggle Ephemeral Mode in Preferences, ensuring that clipboard history is held exclusively in volatile RAM and instantly wiped upon quitting the app.

## 4. System Permissions
- **Accessibility:** Zest requests macOS Accessibility permissions solely to synthesize a `⌘ + V` keystroke into your target application when you select a clip. Zest never logs keystrokes, monitors system typing, or inspects content outside the system clipboard.

## 5. Third-Party Services
- Zest does not integrate with third-party cloud storage, databases, or analytics platforms. In-app purchases are processed directly and securely by Apple via the Mac App Store using StoreKit.
